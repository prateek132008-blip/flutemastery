Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Thank you for purchasing the Flute Mastery Course. Due to the digital nature of our course and the instant delivery of course materials, all sales are generally final. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase.

Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds will only be considered in the following situations:

  • You were charged successfully but did not receive the course access link, login credentials, or course materials due to a delivery issue on our side.
  • A verified technical problem on our side prevents you from accessing or viewing the course content, and the issue cannot be resolved within a reasonable time after you contact support.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:

  • You have successfully received access to the course materials.
  • You have received the course link, files, login credentials, videos, PDFs, or any other course content.
  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Personal preferences or dissatisfaction with the teaching style, content, or course format.
  • Lack of time to complete the course.
  • Failure to use the course after purchase.
  • Purchasing the course by mistake.
  • Any reason other than a genuine delivery or technical-access issue caused by us.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for an eligible issue, contact us within 2 days of purchase and provide:

  • Your full name
  • Razorpay Payment ID
  • A description of the issue
  • Screenshots or evidence of the problem (if applicable)

After reviewing your request, we will determine whether it qualifies under this refund policy.

Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 2–4 business days.
  • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used during purchase.
  • Any payment gateway processing fees may be deducted where applicable.
