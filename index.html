Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How long does it take to learn flute? +
With consistent daily practice of 20–30 minutes, most beginners can play simple melodies within 4–6 weeks of starting this course. To reach an intermediate level where you can play full songs fluently, expect 3–6 months of regular practice. Progress varies by individual — the most important thing is consistency.
Do I need prior experience or music knowledge? +
Absolutely not. This course is designed from the ground up for complete beginners — people who have never played any instrument before. We start from holding the flute correctly and explain everything step-by-step. No music theory background is required.
Can I watch lessons offline? +
Yes! All video lessons can be downloaded for offline viewing. Once downloaded, you can watch them anytime without an internet connection — perfect for practicing during travel or in areas with poor connectivity.
How will I receive my course after purchase? +
Immediately after your payment is confirmed, you'll receive an email with your course access link and instructions IF NOT. You can also contact us on WhatsApp with your payment details and we'll activate your access manually within a few Minutes. All content is hosted on a secure platform you can access from any device.
How does WhatsApp support work? +
After purchasing the Course + Support or Premium plan, you'll be added to a dedicated WhatsApp conversation with your instructor. You can send voice notes, videos of your playing, or text your questions — and receive personalized replies, explanations, or custom short videos addressing your specific issue. Support is available 7 days a week.
How do the live one-on-one classes work? +
The Premium Mentorship plan includes 4 live one-on-one sessions per month conducted via video call (Google Meet / Zoom / WhatsApp video). After purchase, you'll coordinate directly with your instructor to schedule sessions at a time convenient for you. Each session is typically 45–60 minutes, focused entirely on your individual progress and challenges.
Is this course truly beginner friendly? +
Yes — that's the core promise of this program. Every lesson is scripted and paced for someone who has never touched a flute. We use clear Hindi and simple English explanations, demonstrate every technique multiple times, and anticipate the exact mistakes beginners make.
