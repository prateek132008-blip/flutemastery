Course Delivery Policy

How you receive and access your course

How the Course is Delivered

Flute Mastery is a fully digital, online course. After successful payment, you receive instant access to all course materials via a private Google Drive link.

Access Delivery Process

  • Step 1: You complete payment via Razorpay.
  • Step 2: You are instantly redirected to the Thank You page, which contains your course access link.
  • Step 3: An automated email is sent to your email address containing the Google Drive link, PDF notes, and a welcome message.
  • Step 4: Click the Google Drive link to access all course videos and materials immediately.

Access Timeline

Course access is granted immediately upon successful payment. The confirmation email is typically delivered within 5–10 minutes of purchase. If you do not receive the email within 30 minutes, please check your spam/junk folder or contact support directly on WhatsApp.

What You Receive

  • Access to a private Google Drive folder containing all 10+ video lessons
  • Downloadable PDF practice notes and exercises
  • Lifetime access — including all future updates
  • WhatsApp support access for personal guidance

Technical Requirements

  • A stable internet connection to stream video lessons
  • A Google account (free) to access Google Drive
  • Any device: mobile, tablet, or computer

Didn't Receive Your Access?

If you completed payment but did not receive access within 30 minutes:

  • Check your spam/junk email folder
  • Ensure you entered the correct email address during payment
  • Contact us on WhatsApp with your payment ID for immediate assistance
