How you receive and access your course

How the Course is Delivered

Flute Mastery is a fully digital, online course. After payment is made and verified, you receive access to all course materials via a private Google Drive link.

Access Delivery Process

  • Step 1: Scan the UPI QR code shown on our website and pay ₹699 using any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc).
  • Step 2: Take a screenshot of the successful payment.
  • Step 3: Send the payment screenshot to us on WhatsApp.
  • Step 4: Once your payment is verified, we will send you the Google Drive link and PDF notes on WhatsApp (and by email, where provided).

Access Timeline

Course access is granted after your payment screenshot has been verified on WhatsApp, typically within a few hours. If you do not hear back within 30 minutes during our support hours, please follow up with us directly on WhatsApp.

What You Receive

  • Access to a private Google Drive folder containing all 10+ video lessons
  • Downloadable PDF practice notes and exercises
  • Lifetime access — including all future updates
  • WhatsApp support access for personal guidance

Technical Requirements

  • A stable internet connection to stream video lessons
  • A Google account (free) to access Google Drive
  • Any device: mobile, tablet, or computer

Didn't Receive Your Access?

If you completed payment but did not receive access:

  • Check your WhatsApp and spam/junk email folder
  • Make sure you sent your payment screenshot on WhatsApp
  • Contact us on WhatsApp with your payment screenshot for immediate assistance
