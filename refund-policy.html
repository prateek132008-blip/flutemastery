We stand behind the quality of our course. If you are not satisfied, we offer a simple, hassle-free refund policy.

2-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with the Flute Mastery course for any reason, you may request a full refund within 2 days of your purchase date. No questions asked.

How to Request a Refund

  • Contact us via WhatsApp or email within 2 days of purchase.
  • Provide your name and Razorpay payment ID (found in your purchase confirmation email).
  • We will process your refund within 2-4 business days.
  • The refund will be credited to the original payment method used.

Refund Conditions

  • Refund requests must be made within 2 days of the purchase date.
  • After 2 days, no refunds will be issued.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method only.
  • Razorpay processing fees (if applicable) may be deducted in some cases.

*Special Notes

  • Refunds will only be considered if you have not received access to the course due to a delivery issue, or if a technical problem on our side prevents you from accessing or viewing the course content.
  • If you have successfully received the course access link, files, login credentials, or any course materials, you will not be eligible for a refund.
  • Refund requests based on personal preferences, change of mind, lack of usage, or dissatisfaction after accessing the course materials will not be accepted.
  • Once course access has been granted and the course materials have been delivered, the purchase is considered final and non-refundable.
  • If you experience any genuine technical issues preventing access to the course, please contact our support team with relevant details so we can resolve the issue promptly.
